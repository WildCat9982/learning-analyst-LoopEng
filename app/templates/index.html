Review criteria

Completeness
Every requirement clause is addressed in five nonempty sections, in the required order. Missing decisions are left open.
Clarity
Questions name the relevant clause and ask for a concrete decision or measurable boundary. Each ambiguity identifies who or what can resolve it.
Depth
Facts come from the requirement; proposed details are questions or assumptions. Each ambiguity includes a relevant boundary or failure scenario.
Full review criteria
  • completeness: All requirement clauses are addressed in five nonempty level-2 sections, in order: Confirmed Facts, Ambiguities, Clarification Questions, Assumptions, Summary. No missing requirement is silently treated as settled.
  • clarity: Questions name the missing decision and the relevant clause, asking for concrete choices or measurable boundaries. Merely asking what "reasonable", "common", or "secure" means is insufficient. Each ambiguity identifies who or what can settle it; labels alone are insufficient.
  • depth: Confirmed Facts contain only information stated in the requirement. Proposed numbers, policies, formats, or implementations are explicitly questions or assumptions, never invented facts. Each ambiguity includes a relevant concrete boundary/failure scenario; generic boilerplate is insufficient.

The required sections are Confirmed Facts, Ambiguities, Clarification Questions, Assumptions and Summary, using level-2 headings. Labels alone or generic boilerplate do not satisfy the checks.

4 Compare the results Waiting for a decision

What to do Compare the before and after results, then explain what changed.

These checks use each report's first draft, before the agent revises it.

Result target and scoring

For an accepted change: at least 12 of 15 checks pass, or three more pass than before. No criterion total may decline. Keeping the original qualifies only if the baseline already passed at least 12/15 and no criterion total declines.

A qualifying result earns 50 quality points. Otherwise, quality points are 50 × passing after-checks ÷ 15. Missing checks are Not evaluated. A passing course grade needs 70 points, the required saved evidence and a qualifying result; otherwise the total is capped at 69. Partial work remains submittable.

Done when The comparison and your explanation are saved.
