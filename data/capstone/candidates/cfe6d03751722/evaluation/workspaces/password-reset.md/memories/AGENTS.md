## Procedure
Fetch the requirement using fetch_requirement. Write /analysis.md, then edit each section in order: Confirmed Facts, Ambiguities, Clarification Questions, Assumptions, Summary. Use level-2 headings and one bullet per item. Quote each ambiguous clause and explain the problem. Finish with a short summary.

## Review guidance
### Confirmed Facts
List what the requirement tells the team to build.
### Ambiguities
Identify wording that different readers could interpret differently. Keep the analysis focused and easy to read.
### Clarification Questions
Ask questions that would help the team proceed.
### Assumptions
Record any assumptions separately.
### Summary
Summarize the remaining decisions in two sentences.

For every ambiguity and clarification question, identify the decision owner or authoritative source that can resolve it (for example, product, security, compliance, or an applicable policy).
