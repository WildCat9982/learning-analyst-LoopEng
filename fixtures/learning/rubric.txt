- completeness: All requirement clauses are addressed in five nonempty level-2 sections, in order: Confirmed Facts, Ambiguities, Clarification Questions, Assumptions, Summary. No missing requirement is silently treated as settled.
- clarity: Questions name the missing decision and the relevant clause, asking for concrete choices or measurable boundaries. Merely asking what "reasonable", "common", or "secure" means is insufficient. Each ambiguity identifies who or what can settle it; labels alone are insufficient.
- depth: Confirmed Facts contain only information stated in the requirement. Proposed numbers, policies, formats, or implementations are explicitly questions or assumptions, never invented facts. Each ambiguity includes a relevant concrete boundary/failure scenario; generic boilerplate is insufficient.
